§1 General

13pairs

Best of 3sets, match tie-break at 1–1
Playtomicevery match recorded as competitive
  1. The GPPL is a league from our Pakistani community in Germany – open to all padel friends. If more pairs register than there are places by the deadline, full Pakistani pairs get priority.
    • If you have a non-Pakistani partner and would lose your place to a full Pakistani pair, you first get the chance to find a Pakistani partner (in the order of your position in the list) – otherwise the place goes to the next pair on the waiting list.
  2. Season 1 has 13 pairs.
  3. The season lasts approx. 4.5 months (13 matchdays of 10 days): the league and the cup, ending with a finale.
  4. Matches are best of 3 sets and are recorded as competitive matches in Playtomic.
  5. At 1–1 in sets, a match tie-break (first to 10, win by 2) decides. It counts as the 3rd set.
  6. Exception: the GPPC Cup semi-finals and final are played over 3 full sets – no match tie-break (§4).
  7. At 2–0 the match is over. A 3rd set played for fun doesn't count and must not be entered in Playtomic.

§2 League

  1. There is one league. Every pair plays every other pair once – 12 matches. With 13 pairs, each pair has one matchday off.
  2. Matches are played in 10-day matchdays. The start date and the whole schedule – all matchdays and pairings – will be published at once on the Schedule tab.
  3. Pairs should preferably play their matches in the order of the matchday schedule. If there is a scheduling conflict, the matches can be played in any order. Every pair must reach these milestones:

  4. Matches missing at a milestone are treated as walkovers (§6): the pair responsible gets a 1-point deduction – if both pairs are at fault, both do.

§4 GPPC Cup

The German Pakistan Padel Champion Cup – a tribute to GPPC itself.

  1. The GPPC Cup is a knockout cup. Every pair takes part, and rounds are drawn randomly (Pokal style).
  2. The cup is played alongside the second half of the league. Each round has a window of 2–3 league matchdays, and its draw takes place at the start of the window:
    Cup round13 pairs league: 13 matchdays
    Round 1Matchdays 6–86 matches + 1 random bye
    Quarter-finalsMatchdays 9–106 winners + the bye + 1 lucky loser
    Semi-finalsMatchdays 11–133 full sets
    FinalAt the finaleno draw
  3. Round 1: every pair plays. With an odd number of pairs, one pair gets a random bye.
  4. Where applicable, lucky losers drawn from the round 1 losers fill up the quarter-finals. They aren't needed when round 1 already leaves exactly 8 pairs (e.g. with 16 pairs).
  5. The quarter-finals and semi-finals are drawn randomly again.
  6. There is no draw for the final – the two semi-final winners meet.
  7. Every draw is decided by seed words: 3 randomly chosen people – participants or not – each give one word, and the words are revealed at the same time. Anyone can check a draw on the Verify draw tab.
  8. Cup round 1 and the quarter-finals are played like league matches (match tie-break at 1–1). The semi-finals and the final are 3 full sets – book 2 hours.
  9. Courts for cup matches are booked as for league matches (§5), except the final (§8).
  10. A cup match must be played within its window. Otherwise, the pair responsible is out of the cup (walkover). If both pairs are at fault, both are out: the next draw gives a bye for the free place – and in a semi-final, the other semi-final winner wins the cup.

§5 Courts

DefaultWellpass court, indoorsDate, time and place agreed by both pairs – indoors from November to March.
ExceptionPaid courtOnly if both pairs agree (costs split equally), or the pair suggesting it pays the full court.
  1. By default, matches are played on Wellpass courts. Date and time are agreed by both pairs.
  2. A paid court is only used if both pairs agree (costs split equally), or if the pair suggesting it pays the full court.
  3. From November to March, matches are played on indoor courts, unless all 4 players agree to play outdoors.
  4. Both pairs agree on the court location. If they can't, they choose a court with roughly equal travel time for both pairs.
  5. If there's still no agreement, the organizers decide. Their decision is final – refusing it counts as not playing (§6).

§6 Postponements & walkovers

Home-country tripLong business tripInjuryIllness
Walkover

  1. A match may only be postponed for a home-country trip, a long business trip (covering at least 50% of the matchday window), injury or illness.
  2. The organizers must be informed as soon as possible.
  3. Milestones and the end of the league are hard deadlines.
  4. In case of a walkover, the opponent gets 3 points and the pair responsible gets a 1-point deduction.
  5. If both pairs are responsible for the match not being played, both get a 1-point deduction.
  6. A walkover counts as 2–0 in sets and 0–0 in games.
  7. Walkovers are not entered in Playtomic – the organizers record them.

§7 Replacements & withdrawals

Replacement playerInjury, illness or unplanned home tripSimilar level, approved by the organizers. At least one original player plays.
WithdrawalFree until 24 h after the registration deadlineAfter that: find a new partner – or the waiting list / a replacement team takes the place.
  1. A replacement player is only allowed for injury, illness or an unplanned trip to the home country.
  2. The replacement must be of similar level and approved by the organizers in due time.
  3. At least one original player of the pair must play.
  4. A replacement can't be a player from another league pair.
  5. A complete pair can withdraw within 24 hours after the registration deadline and gets the entry fee back (§14.8). After that, the entry fee is not refunded.
  6. After that, if one player can't continue, they find another partner.
  7. If the whole pair withdraws, the next pair on the waiting list takes their place. If there is none, the pair must find their own replacement team.
  8. Replacement players must be of similar level. Replacement players and teams must be approved by the organizers.

§9 Matchday

📸BeforeTeam photo of all 4 players, posted in the GPPL group

📲AfterResult in Playtomic and in 🎾 My team on the website

  1. Before each match, take a photo of all 4 players together on court and post it in the GPPL WhatsApp group before the match starts.
  2. Without the photo, the match isn't counted. It counts as not played and must be played again within the matchday window or, at the latest, before the next milestone (§6 applies). If it isn't played again in time, both pairs get a 1-point deduction.
  3. If a replacement player plays (§7), they must be in the photo – it shows who actually played.
  4. The match is played as described in §1 (league) or §4 (cup).
  5. After the match, the result is entered in Playtomic and on the website (§11).

§10 Playtomic & scheduling

  1. Every player needs a Playtomic account with a level before the season starts, and must keep it for the whole duration of the league.
  2. Every league and cup match is recorded as a competitive match in Playtomic, with the 4 players who actually played.
  3. Each pair logs in to 🎾 My team on the website with its team code (sent privately by the organizers).
  4. Once both pairs have agreed on a match, one of them enters the date, time, duration and venue in 🎾 My team. It can be changed or removed there until the match has a result.
  5. Dates, times and venues are not public – only the two pairs of the match and the organizers see them. The public website only shows the pairings and confirmed results.
  6. A 3rd set played for fun (§1.7) and walkovers (§6.7) are never entered in Playtomic.
  7. Competitive matches change your Playtomic level – that's part of playing in the league.

§11 Results & disputes

Entering the result

  1. After the match, one of the two pairs enters the result within 48 hours – in Playtomic (as a competitive match) and in 🎾 My team. The match date must have been set first (§10.4).
  2. A result in 🎾 My team needs the score, the matchday photo (§9) and the Playtomic link of the match.
  3. The score is entered set by set, in games. A set ends 6–0 … 6–4, 7–5 or 7–6. At 1–1 in sets, the match tie-break is entered as the 3rd set (first to 10, win by 2 – e.g. 10–7 or 12–10). Cup semi-finals and the final have a full 3rd set (§4). The website only accepts possible scores.
  4. Until the opponents react, the pair that entered the result can correct or withdraw it.

Confirming or disputing

  1. The other pair confirms or disputes the result – in Playtomic and in 🎾 My team. If it doesn't react within 48 hours, the result counts as confirmed.
  2. A pair that disputes a result can add a comment explaining why. It helps the organizers, but isn't required.
  3. A disputed result is decided by the organizers, who check the Playtomic match and the photo and may correct the score. Their decision is final.
  4. Results waiting for confirmation or disputed are not public – only the two pairs and the organizers see them.

Official results

  1. Only confirmed results count – for the league table, the milestones (§2) and the cup.
  2. If no result has been entered within 48 hours of the match, the pairs must contact the organizers. How the match is counted is then at the organizers' discretion.
  3. The organizers record walkovers (§6) and can enter or correct a result at any time, e.g. after a mistake.

§14 Terms & conditions

Registration & eligibility

  1. By registering, every player accepts this rulebook, including these terms.
  2. Players must be at least 18 years old, or have the consent of a parent or guardian.
  3. Each player can only be registered in one pair.
  4. The registration deadline is one week after the 8th pair has registered. The exact date is then announced on the website.
  5. A registration is only valid once the entry fee has been paid – the time of payment counts as the time of registration. Places are given first come, first served, with the priority rule in §1.1.
  6. A player who registers without a partner finds a partner themselves. The partner must be registered at the latest 1 day before the schedule is published – the publishing date is announced at least 1–2 days in advance. If no partner is found by then, the registration ends and the entry fee is refunded.
  7. Until that deadline, the player can ask the organizers for an extension – this needs a good reason. The organizers decide, based on the nature of the reason, whether to grant it and for how long, and they can also decline it. With an extension, the team gets its place in the schedule – but if there is still no partner when the extension ends, the entry fee is not refunded and all of the team's matches count as walkovers (§6).
  8. A pair that is complete at the registration deadline can still opt out within 24 hours after the deadline – the entry fee is refunded. After that, the entry fee is no longer refunded.
  9. One organizer pair can register even after the registration deadline and even if the maximum number of pairs has been reached – at the latest 1 day before the schedule is published. The draw and the schedule are not delayed to accommodate them. Organizer pairs pay the entry fee like every other pair.
  10. Until 1 day before the schedule is published, the two players of a registered pair can split up and each join a new pair, for example with a player still looking for a partner – with the organizers' approval. Both new pairs must be complete by that deadline. The original pair's entry fee carries over to one of the new pairs; the other new pair pays its own entry fee.
  11. Team names must be respectful. The organizers can ask a pair to change an offensive or inappropriate name.
  12. If fewer than 8 pairs register, the season doesn't take place and all entry fees are refunded.

On and off court

  1. Fair play: respect your opponents, the rules and the clubs. Players call the balls on their own side – if in doubt, the point is replayed.
  2. Be on time. If a pair is more than 15 minutes late without notice, the opponent can claim a walkover. The organizers decide.
  3. If a match can't be finished in the booked court time because a pair – or one of its players – arrived late, the opponent gets a walkover (§6).
  4. Every player brings their own racket. As a courtesy, the pair that doesn't book the court brings the balls – in good condition, or bought on the spot (most clubs have a ball machine). The organizers provide new balls for the cup semi-finals and finals.
  5. The house rules of each club apply. Cancellation fees and damage are paid by whoever caused them.
  6. If a court becomes unavailable (e.g. closure or a booking problem), this counts as a valid reason to postpone (§6).
  7. The organizers can exclude players or pairs for unsporting behaviour. The entry fee is not refunded.

Money

  1. The entry fee is €30 per pair. It goes towards running the league and the GPPC Cup – including prizes, balls for the cup semi-finals and finals, and the finale – and is managed by the organizers. It is not refunded later than 24 hours after the registration deadline (§14.8).
  2. If the season has to be stopped early, entry fees are only refunded if the organizers are at fault. Otherwise, the organizers decide how any remaining money is used.

Member area (🎾 My team)

  1. Each pair gets a personal team code for 🎾 My team. It is only for the two players of the pair – don't share it with anyone else – anyone with it can set dates and enter or confirm results for your team.
  2. If your code gets lost or into the wrong hands, tell the organizers straight away. They reset it, and the old code stops working on every device.
  3. Only enter dates and results that are correct. Results entered wrongly on purpose, or other misuse of the member area, can lead to a point deduction, losing access or exclusion from the league – the organizers decide.
  4. Every change in the member area is logged and can be seen by the organizers.

Safety, data & organizers

  1. Participation is at your own risk. The organizers are not liable for injuries, lost or damaged belongings, or court costs. Every player is responsible for their own health and insurance.
  2. Data: team names, player names, links to the players' Playtomic profiles and results are shown publicly on the website. Contact details are only used by the organizers to run the league and are deleted after the season.
  3. Official communication happens in the GPPL WhatsApp group and on the website. Players must be reachable there.
  4. If an organizer plays in a match that needs a decision, another organizer or a neutral person decides.
  5. For anything this rulebook doesn't cover, the official rules of the International Padel Federation (FIP) apply.
  6. In situations neither this rulebook nor the FIP rules cover, the organizers decide. Their decision is final.
  7. The organizers may adjust the rules during the season if it becomes necessary. Changes will be communicated through the proper channels.
